## STEP-BY-STEP MINTING GUIDE ON OPENSEA

### Before You Start — What You Need
- [ ] MetaMask wallet (or Coinbase Wallet) installed in your browser
- [ ] A small amount of ETH in your wallet for gas fees (approx. $10–$30 USD per NFT on Ethereum, or near $0 on Polygon)
- [ ] All 4 NFT image files ready on your computer
- [ ] This document open for copy-pasting names, descriptions, and traits

> **Tip on chain:** If you want to avoid gas fees, choose **Polygon** when creating your collection. The NFTs will still appear on OpenSea and can be listed for sale. If you want maximum prestige/value, choose **Ethereum**.

---

### STEP 1 — Create the Collection

1. Go to **[opensea.io](https://opensea.io)** and click **Connect Wallet** (top right).
2. Connect your MetaMask or preferred wallet and sign the message.
3. Click your profile icon (top right) → **Studio**.
4. Click **Create a Collection**.
5. Fill in the collection details:

| Field | What to Enter |
|-------|--------------|
| **Name** | The Bloom Society — Wealth Sigils |
| **URL** | thebloomsociety-wealth-sigils *(or similar, must be unique)* |
| **Description** | Four eternal wealth sigils from The Bloom Society — each one a permanent on-chain invocation of prosperity from the world's most powerful wealth traditions: Roman, Vedic, Nordic, and Chinese. Ownership is immutable. Power is scarce. |
| **Category** | Art |
| **Blockchain** | Ethereum *or* Polygon |

6. Upload a **collection logo image** (use one of the NFT images or your Bloom Society logo).
7. Upload a **banner image** (use one of the NFT images in landscape crop).
8. Click **Create** and sign the transaction in your wallet.

---

Once all 4 are minted, your collection page will show all 4 NFTs with their traits visible. To make them visible to buyers:

2. Share the collection link: `opensea.io/collection/thebloomsociety-wealth-sigils`
3. Update your homepage OpenSea link to point directly to the collection URL.

---

### How Rarity Shows Up on OpenSea

OpenSea calculates rarity **automatically** based on how many NFTs in your collection share the same trait value. Since you have:
- 2 NFTs with `Rarity: Legendary` → they show as rarer
- 2 NFTs with `Rarity: Epic` → slightly more common

The `Rarity` property you add manually will also display clearly on each item's page under **Properties**. OpenSea also shows a **Rarity Score** tab on each item once the collection has enough items.
